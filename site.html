How we work safely

AI does the work. People keep control.

Your code and data stay yours

Code, data and system knowledge belong to you, and are handed over on the terms set in our contract.

Agents hold no credentials

AI agents never hold passwords, keys or tokens. Access is limited to what each job needs.

Production needs a person

No change reaches production without the approval of a named engineer.

Verification is independent

The agent that did the work never approves its own result.

Everything leaves a record

Each outcome keeps its evidence: what was asked, what was changed and how it was checked.

A person answers for incidents

If something goes wrong, you deal with a named engineer, not with a tool.
